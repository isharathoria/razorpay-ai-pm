01

Problem

Knowledge was scattered

Onboarding a new US healthcare system at Qure.ai means navigating product, technical, integration, deployment and process documentation that lives in many places.

Experts became the bottleneck

New team members leaned on experienced colleagues to find the right answer. The same questions repeated, senior time was pulled away, and onboarding slowed down.

02

Opportunity: why RAG, not a generic LLM

The answers people needed were company-specific, current and contextual, which a general-purpose model doesn't know. Retrieval-augmented generation lets the agent pull from approved internal sources first, so answers stay grounded in what Qure.ai actually does.

  • Grounded: answers come from approved internal docs, not model guesswork.
  • Current: update the knowledge base and the answers change with it. No retraining.
  • Traceable: the relevant context is surfaced with the answer, so users can verify it.
03

Solution: a retrieval-grounded onboarding assistant

Team members ask onboarding questions in plain language. The agent retrieves the right internal knowledge, the LLM answers from it, and users troubleshoot and navigate complex documentation without waiting on a subject-matter expert.

07

Product learnings

Context beats cleverness. AI is only useful when it is grounded in the right context.
Retrieval is half the product. Retrieval quality can matter as much as model quality.
Eval on real tasks. Test against the questions users actually ask, not model benchmarks.
Fit the workflow. The best AI tools fit into how teams already work and don't ask them to change habits.
